- **Archives card in Stats**
  A new Archives card lists the views a site's archive pages received, grouped by what they archive: search terms, categories, tags, authors, "other", and any custom taxonomy the site has. Each group expands to show the individual pages, and tapping one opens it in the browser. The card is not shown by default — add it from "Add card" at the bottom of the Traffic tab.
  - **Apps:** Both
  - **Type:** New
  - **Availability:** Flag: android_new_stats
  - **Where:** My Site → Stats → Traffic → Add card → Archives
  - **Notes:** Groups are ranked by total views and each lists its busiest pages; a site with thousands of search terms will not see every one of them.

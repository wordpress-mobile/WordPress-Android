- **Archives card in Stats**
  A new Archives card lists the views a site's archive pages received, grouped by what they archive: search terms, categories, tags, authors, "other", and one group for the terms of all the custom taxonomies the site has. Each group expands to show the individual pages, and tapping one opens it in the browser. The card is not shown by default — add it from "Add card" at the bottom of the Traffic tab.
  - **Apps:** Both
  - **Type:** New
  - **Availability:** Flag: android_new_stats
  - **Where:** My Site → Stats → Traffic → Add card → Archives
  - **Notes:** Groups are ranked by total views. The card lists each group's ten busiest pages and "View more" opens the rest, so a site with thousands of search terms sees its top ones rather than all of them.
